What is Financial Independence?

Financial Independence (FI) is the tipping point where your investments generate enough passive income to cover your living expenses indefinitely. You no longer have to work for money—giving you the ultimate freedom to design your time and life. It's the opportunity you've been waiting for - to tell your boss or your clients to go to hell and to know that you'll be just fine.

This simulator helps you visualize that finish line. By mapping out your current savings rate, investment choices, and desired future lifestyle, you can see exactly when you will cross the FI threshold—and discover which levers to pull to get there sooner.

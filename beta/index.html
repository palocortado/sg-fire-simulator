What is Financial Independence?

Financial Independence (FI) is the tipping point where your investments generate enough passive income to cover your living expenses indefinitely. You no longer have to work for money—giving you the ultimate freedom to design your time and life. It's the opportunity you've been waiting for - to tell your boss or your clients to go to hell and to know that you'll be just fine.

This simulator helps you visualize that finish line. By mapping out your current savings rate, investment choices, and desired future lifestyle, you can see exactly when you will cross the FI threshold—and discover which levers to pull to get there sooner.

Choose SGD if you use a local robo-advisor (Syfe, StashAway, Endowus), hold SGD-hedged funds or unit trusts, invest via CPFIS or SRS, hold an investment-linked policy quoted in SGD, or aren't sure.

Choose USD if you buy USD-listed ETFs or stocks through a brokerage such as Interactive Brokers, moomoo, Tiger or Saxo (for example VWRA, IWDA, CSPX or individual US shares). Also choose USD if you hold a USD-denominated unit trust or a US 401(k) or IRA from working overseas, or if you fund your investments from USD income.

In USD mode, the engine converts your balance into SGD at today's exchange rate, then applies currency movements each year. Historically, the SGD has strengthened against the USD, which has reduced returns for Singapore investors in global markets.
